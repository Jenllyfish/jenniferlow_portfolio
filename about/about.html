HEY! I'M JEN!

Jennifer Low is a digital illustrator and graphic designer based in San Jose, California. Following her childhood in the Central Valley, she is currently pursuing a Digital Media Arts BFA at San Jose State University. Jennifer draws inspiration from the past, utilizing nostalgic relics as a springboard for new ideas. Her work blends the technicolor pigments of twentieth century poster design, the whimsically retro illustration style of Global Village Coffeehouse, and the modern terror of liminal spaces. Whether it’s a vintage inspired promotional flyer or a glimpse into a world that doesn’t exist, Jennifer aims to depict vibrantly haunting expressions of human emotion, while paying homage to what she loves.
